The water report

“Crystal clear” is the whole point of this trip, so the research scored expected August water clarity per destination. Resorts rake their sand every morning — nobody can rake the sea.

Seaweed year from hell. USF’s June 30 bulletin measured 33.6 million metric tons — the second-worst on record — and expects beaching to keep increasing through August. Punta Cana, Cancún’s hotel zone, Barbados and Puerto Rico are the hit zones.
The shelters. Cozumel’s west coast, Grand Cayman’s Seven Mile Beach and Negril sit outside or shielded from the belt. Costa Mujeres faces northwest and was reported light in July.
Hurricanes: green light. Quietest forecast since 2014 (9 named storms vs a 14 average; NOAA 55% below-normal). Our week also lands well before the mid-September peak.
Day-to-day honesty: beach-level seaweed is wind-dependent. The scores are odds, not promises — which is exactly why Cozumel’s physical shelter is worth a premium if clear water is non-negotiable.
